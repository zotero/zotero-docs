# Does Zotero support institutional single sign on?
We do not currently support any institutional single sign on or account provisioning using systems like LDAP or Active Directory.

Each Zotero account belongs to the user that registered it, and is covered by our [terms of service](/support/terms/terms_of_service) and [privacy policy](/support/privacy), even if it has storage supplemented by a Zotero Institution subscription. Users can bring Zotero accounts that they already had, as well as keep the Zotero account and their data after your subscription ends or they leave your institution.
