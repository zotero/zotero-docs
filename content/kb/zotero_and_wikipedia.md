# Zotero and Wikipedia/Wikidata

Zotero can be a powerful tool for Wikipedia and Wikidata users, be they casual visitors or regular contributors. By incorporating print-based references, Wikipedia users are able lend greater credibility to their articles and provide readers with a broader reference base, forging new relationships between online and offline resources. In addition, tools allowing import/export relationships between Zotero and the Wikipedia ecosystem are promising to allow better circulation of information through different scholarly communication networks.

## Wikipedia and Zotero
### Importing references of a Wikipedia article into Zotero
Some [Wikipedia citation templates embed COinS tags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Templates_generating_COinS) in each bibliographic entry—see, for example, the raw HTML markup of the [Wikipedia article for Zotero](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zotero). Therefore, Zotero can import references from articles that employ them. Zotero can also recognize academic references having a DOI.

Tutorial : [Adding the references of a Wikipedia article to your Zotero library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources_with_Zotero#Adding_the_references_of_a_Wikipedia_article_to_your_Zotero_library)

### Using Zotero to generate citations and references Wikipedia
Wikipedia does not have a single [standard citation style](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources#Citation_style) but use various [Citation Templates](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citation_templates) to generate references. Zotero natively include a citation style following a Wikipedia citation template. 

 Tutorials : [Adding references into Wikipedia articles using your text editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources_with_Zotero#Adding_references_into_Wikipedia_articles_using_your_text_editor), [Adding a bibliography to a Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources_with_Zotero#Adding_a_bibliography_to_a_Wikipedia_article)

#### Quick Copy
Zotero's Quick Copy feature makes it easy to export Zotero items to Wikipedia. Open the [Export](preferences/export) pane of Zotero preferences and select "Wikipedia Citation Templates" as the Default Format. Then, you can drag and drop items from your library to the Wikipedia to insert properly formatted citations. You can also copy Wikipedia Citation Templates data to your clipboad by pressing Ctrl/Cmd-Shift-C.
*N.B.: if you wish to cite a web page in Wikipedia you must include a date in the accessed field.*

See: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources_with_Zotero>

## Wikidata and Zotero
Wikidata is a sister project of Wikipedia, operated by the same non-profit organisation (the Wikimedia Foundation), in the same volunteer-edited manner. It provides linked, open data about the entities described in Wikidata, and many more besides, under a Public Domain licence.

### Importing bibliographic data into Wikidata
- For batch item creation, use Zotero and the plugin [Cita](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Zotero/Cita): Cita fetches existing QID for items to avoid creating duplicates in Wikidata > then Cita proposes the creation of new item with a directly (with an authentification token) or through Quickstatement

- For single item creation (for items with a DOI), use [BHL](https://bhl2wiki.herokuapp.com/) or [Scholia](https://scholia.toolforge.org/id-to-quickstatements): copy the DOI in the tool and run the command in Quickstatement

### Adding a Wikidata item to Zotero
To add a Wikidata item (e.g. Book, article) into Zotero, use the [Zotero connector](https://www.zotero.org/download/connectors) and click on the connector button to add the item into your library.

For details, see : <https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Zotero> and <https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Sources>
