# Can I update the email address on my account or add a backup?
You can add backup email addresses, or update your primary email, from the [email settings](/settings/email). In order to update your primary email address, the new email must be verified by clicking the link we send when you add it. Access the verification link using the browser where you're logged in to the account.

To ensure continued access to your Zotero account, we recommend adding a backup email address, especially if your primary email is a work or institutional email that may change in the future.

Any verified email address can be used to identify your account when logging in, to verify your identity for 2-step/multifactor log in, or to recover your password.
